**What I'll do**:

4. Tell me why you like them

---

### Phase 2: Screenshot & Annotate (You drive)
**What you do**:
1. Take screenshots of designs you love
2. Save them to a folder
3. Annotate with notes:
   - "Love this color scheme"
   - "Great card shadow depth"
   - "Perfect spacing"
   - "Nice typography hierarchy"

**What I do**:
1. Analyze your selections
2. Extract common patterns
3. Identify your style preferences

---

### Phase 3: Theme Generation (I create)
**What I'll do based on your selections**:
1. Extract color palette
2. Define typography scale
3. Specify spacing system
4. Document component styles
5. Create design tokens (Swift code)
6. Provide example mockups in ASCII/text

**Output**: Complete design system document with code

---

### Phase 4: Visual Mockups (We iterate)
**Two options**:

**Option A: Text-based mockups** (I create in markdown)
- Detailed ASCII art layouts
- Color descriptions
- Component specifications
- Quick to iterate

**Option B: You create in Figma/Sketch** (Based on my specs)
- I provide exact specifications
- You design visual mockups
- We iterate until perfect
